# Homepage and shared public website

Mode: Persuade on landing pages; Read on policy, research and archive pages.

## Direction contract
Approved code-first implementation. Existing NOCSC brand is visual authority; no concept tournament is needed to replace it. Use a light daytime streetscape with strong blue typography and yellow calls to action. Dense, sturdy Barlow Condensed headings relate directly to the supplied mark; full-width Barlow makes long text readable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: `.home-hero` carries the complete-streets mission, real local street photography, and `.hero-action` leading to participation. No eyebrow above the heading. Desktop uses a generous headline beside the photograph; mobile puts the message and action before imagery. No text overlays on busy photographs.

STORY: Mission → explanation of a complete street → policy → dated 2025 grades → current campaigns → meeting invitation. Each action opens a real page or a confirmed destination. Resource pages carry breadcrumbs, a short introduction, clear lists, and source links. Images are evidence, not filler. StoryMap is the primary project map; Bike Streets is a separate route-finding tool.

Motion: one hero entrance, restrained hover/press states and a photo-led moment. Reduced motion leaves all content visible. No scroll-jacking, repeated reveal effects or decorative dashboard UI.

Approved content requirements override commercial landing-page rules in the taste kit: real full meeting address, supplied map iframes, report grades, useful reading length, and natural-height resource pages. Preserve the source kit unchanged and report check limitations honestly.
