## Plan access from the beginning
The coalition's Complete Streets Event Partnership helps organizers consider transportation before an event takes place. Our checklist brings access, communication and neighborhood conditions into the planning conversation.

1. **Review your location.** Consider sidewalks, crossings, transit stops, bike parking, shade and accessible entrances.
2. **Share practical travel information.** Include walking, bicycling and transit options in invitations, alongside any necessary parking information.
3. **Talk with the coalition.** Share your event plan and completed checklist. We can discuss access gaps, opportunities to collaborate and the next steps toward recognition.
4. **Learn from the day.** Ask attendees how they arrived and what could make the trip easier next time.

Submitting a checklist does not automatically certify an event. Recognition is coordinated with the coalition.

## A first partnership
The Urban Conservancy's *Cool People Who Know Things* event in July 2026 helped pilot this approach. One lesson was simple: consider routes and access early enough to include them in event communications.

## Useful things to share
Link attendees to [Bike Streets' New Orleans map](/resources/bike-routes/) and [RTA trip planning](https://www.norta.com/). Check the actual entrance and nearby crossings yourself, and tell attendees who to contact with access questions.
