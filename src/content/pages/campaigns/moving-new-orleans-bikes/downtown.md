**Historical guide · 2021 planning and construction updates**

In 2021, Moving New Orleans Bikes proposed these connections and street improvements.

## Corridors in the original guide

| Corridor | Improvements described at the time |
| --- | --- |
| Elysian Fields, N. Peters–St. Claude | Parking-protected bikeways, crossings and bus-stop access. |
| Franklin, N. Peters–St. Claude | Bicycle boulevard markings and improved crossings. |
| N. Peters, Decatur–Elysian Fields | Protected bikeways and transit-stop improvements. |
| Royal, Franklin–Esplanade | Bicycle boulevard treatments and intersection improvements. |
