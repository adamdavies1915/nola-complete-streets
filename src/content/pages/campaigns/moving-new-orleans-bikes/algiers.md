**Historical guide · 2021 planning and construction updates**

In 2021, Moving New Orleans Bikes proposed these connections and street improvements.

## Corridors in the original guide

| Corridor | Improvements described at the time |
| --- | --- |
| Garden Oaks | Protected lanes, curb ramps and bus-stop repairs. |
| Lawrence and Odeon | Repaving, accessible ramps and crossing improvements. |
| Newton | Protected bike lanes and changes to parking. |
| Wall | Bike lanes, trail connections and transit crossings. |
| MacArthur, Holiday and Westbend | Bikeway proposals connecting neighborhood destinations. |
| Mardi Gras Boulevard | A proposed two-way bicycle route. |
