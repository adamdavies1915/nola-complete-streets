## What, where and why it matters
The project inventory brings transportation projects together so neighbors can find out what is being considered and which agency to follow. The coalition's storymap is the starting point for exploring the work geographically.

A point on a map does not mean construction has started. Check the linked agency page for the project's stage, funding and most recent public update.
