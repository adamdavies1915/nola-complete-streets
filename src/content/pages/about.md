Our streets connect more than destinations. They connect us to jobs, school, friends, the corner store and the culture that makes this city home.

The New Orleans Complete Streets Coalition brings community voices, research and advocacy together. We help people understand what a complete street can be, support neighborhood action and push for public commitments to become everyday improvements.
