## Your information
This website does not have an account system or a contact form that stores submissions. Contact links open your email application. The coalition receives information you choose to send by email.

## Maps and other websites
The Bike Streets map is provided by Bike Streets and loads from its website. If enabled, the project storymap loads from ArcGIS. These services receive ordinary browser connection information when their embeds load and have their own privacy policies. External links, including Better Streets, take you to separately operated websites.

The hosting provider may keep standard server access logs. This site does not add advertising pixels or analytics scripts.

## Accessible participation
We aim to make these pages usable with a keyboard, assistive technology and different screen sizes. The site respects reduced-motion preferences and provides direct links alongside embedded maps. Third-party maps and older PDF documents may have their own limitations.

If a page, document or meeting format creates a barrier for you, [contact the coalition](mailto:info@nolacompletestreets.org) and tell us what would help. You can also ask for information in another format.
