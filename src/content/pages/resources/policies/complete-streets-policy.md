## What is the policy?
New Orleans' Complete Streets ordinance is codified in City Code Section 146-36. The October 2020 Chief Administrative Office Policy Memorandum 134(R) describes how City departments should implement a complete streets approach.

The ordinance and memo are related documents: one establishes the policy in City law; the other describes administrative goals and responsibilities. Read the original documents for their full language and scope.

## What should it do?
The memo connects street decisions to nine goals: safety, connectivity, evaluation, multimodal accommodation, livable communities, equity, coordination, sustainability and resiliency, and economic development.

Its implementation provisions address coordination, public engagement, evaluation and reporting. These are the tools that help turn a general commitment into decisions that can be explained and reviewed.

## What does the coalition look for?
- **A clear owner.** Which department or official is responsible for the next step?
- **Meaningful participation.** Were affected residents involved early enough to influence the design?
- **Visible decisions.** Are trade-offs and policy exceptions explained?
- **Measurable progress.** Is there a public record of what was built, maintained and improved?
