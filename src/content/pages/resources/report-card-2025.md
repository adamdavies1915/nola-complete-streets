## The 2025 grades

| Area | Grade |
| --- | --- |
| Walking & bicycling | C− |
| Transit | C+ |
| Other complete streets priorities | B− |

The **2025 report** grades the City’s progress using the reporting years listed below.

[Download the full 2025 report card (PDF)](/downloads/nocsc-report-card-2025.pdf)

## A few measures behind the grades

| Measure in the report | Finding | Source year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bus stops connected to a sidewalk | 68% | RIDE, 2024 |
| Transit stops with shelters | 15% | RIDE, 2024 |
| Signalized intersections with pedestrian signals at all crossings | 14% | UNOTI, 2025 |
| Intersections with ADA ramps | 54% | City of New Orleans, 2023 |

The full report explains the data sources and additional measures. A connected trip depends on these pieces working together: a stop, the route to it and a crossing that people can use.

## Use it to start a conversation
Ask which projects address the gaps, how investment reaches communities with the greatest need and when the public can expect a progress update. Bring a specific street or trip into the discussion.
