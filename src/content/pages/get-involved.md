## Start with what you know
Tell us about the crossing you avoid, the bus stop that needs shade or the route that helps you get to work. Local knowledge helps us ask better questions and choose meaningful projects.

### Join a campaign
Help with community outreach, event planning, design, data or a neighborhood demonstration. There are ways to contribute with different schedules and skills.

[See current campaigns](/campaigns/current/)

### Bring complete streets to your event
Think through how people will get there, share routes and make it easier to arrive on foot, by bike, using a mobility device or on transit.

[Host a complete streets event](/get-involved/certified-events/)
