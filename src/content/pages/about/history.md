## 2010–2015 · The roots of the coalition
The Sustainable Transportation Advisory Committee brought technical expertise and community perspectives to City Council transportation decisions. Its work helped advance the City's 2011 Complete Streets ordinance, alongside efforts on transit, walking and bicycling.

## 2016 · A coalition takes shape
Members of that committee formed the New Orleans Complete Streets Coalition to support an updated policy and its implementation. Health advocates, transportation organizations and neighborhood groups came together around safer streets, equity and public accountability.

## 2018 · Showing what is possible
Connect the Crescent brought temporary street improvements to New Orleans. Demonstrations gave people a chance to experience safer connections and helped turn a conversation about design into something tangible.

## 2019–2021 · Building support
The coalition brought organizations and neighbors together around safer transportation, supported Moving New Orleans Bikes and helped community groups lead complete streets activations. The City issued an updated Complete Streets Policy Memorandum in October 2020. The coalition joined the City’s Complete Streets Working Group, whose first meeting took place in May 2021.

## 2022 · A shared direction
Members adopted the vision that still guides our work: no matter how you get around, New Orleans streets should be safe, sustainable and accessible for all. A strategic plan set priorities for advocacy, implementation and education.

## 2023–2024 · Following through
Coalition members worked on St. Claude safety, transit access, public engagement and the City's Complete Streets Working Group. Walking and bicycling audits helped bring street conditions into conversations with decision makers.

## 2025 · Measuring the gap
The coalition published a Complete Streets Report Card to bring data on walking, bicycling, transit and other priorities into one public resource. Daylighting became a focus for neighborhood action.

## 2026 · From ideas to action
Current work includes a neighborhood daylighting demonstration, complete streets event partnerships, policy implementation and a public inventory of transportation projects. A report card update is in development.
