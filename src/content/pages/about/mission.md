## Our mission
The New Orleans Complete Streets Coalition mission is to enhance health, equity, sustainability, prosperity, and quality-of-life for the people of New Orleans through advocacy and action aimed at the improvement of our roadways and building of safe transportation options, led by a collaborative membership of organizations and individuals.

## Our vision
**No matter how you get around, New Orleans streets are safe, sustainable, and accessible for all.**

## What guides our work
### Safety and access
Everyone deserves to reach the places they need to go. We advocate for streets that accommodate people of different ages and abilities, including people walking, using mobility devices, bicycling, taking transit and driving.

### Equity and community voice
People who experience a street every day should help shape its future. We work toward investment and engagement that respond to communities whose transportation needs have been overlooked.

### Sustainability and resilience
Shade, trees, drainage, green infrastructure and reliable transportation are connected. We consider how streets can support people through heat, rain and a changing climate.

### Collaboration and accountability
We bring different kinds of expertise to the same table. We follow through on public commitments, ask useful questions and share what we learn.

## Three ways we work
Our 2022–2025 strategic plan organized the coalition around **advocacy, ordinance implementation and education**. Those priorities connect public policy to neighborhood experience.
