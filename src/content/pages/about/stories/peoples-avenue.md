**Originally published July 28, 2021 · Historical summary**

The Edgewood Park Neighborhood Association built a temporary protected space for walking and bicycling on Peoples Avenue. The project responded to neighborhood concerns about speeding and illegal dumping on a street without a sidewalk.

Volunteers helped create a shared path, showing how residents could turn a local concern into a visible demonstration. Association member Orville Lowery described the work as a community effort to improve bicycle access and address traffic problems.

This story records the 2021 demonstration; it does not describe the path’s current condition. Today’s [daylighting campaign](/campaigns/daylighting/) continues the practice of working with neighbors on visible street improvements.
