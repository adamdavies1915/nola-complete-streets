**Originally published December 9, 2021 · Historical summary**

Coalition members shared their work at the American Planning Association’s Louisiana conference in Lafayette in November 2021. The session brought together public agencies, researchers and advocates to discuss the bikeway network and the partnerships behind it.

Tara Tolford of the UNO Transportation Institute discussed using walking and bicycling data to evaluate investments and equity. Bike Easy’s Laura Harris described neighborhood canvassing, mini-grants, business briefings and community rides. City and RTA representatives connected those efforts to planning and implementation.

The discussion highlighted a continuing coalition priority: combine lived experience with research, then use both to support better public decisions. Explore the [research directory](/resources/research/) for tools that support that work today.
