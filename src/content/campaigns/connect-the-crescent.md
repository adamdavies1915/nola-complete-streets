Connect the Crescent used temporary street improvements to demonstrate more comfortable walking and bicycling connections. The project helped make conversations about street design concrete: people could try a route and respond to what they experienced.

The coalition continues to build on the value of demonstrations, neighborhood participation and a connected network.
