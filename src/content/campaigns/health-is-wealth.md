The coalition partnered with the New Orleans Regional Black Chamber of Commerce and Bike Easy to connect safer transportation with health and economic opportunity in the Black community.

## Bike parking at local businesses
The initiative aimed to provide donated bicycle racks to ten Black-owned businesses. The original campaign featured a rack at PJ’s Coffee on Read Boulevard in New Orleans East as an example of making everyday destinations easier to reach by bike.

## A wider conversation
A partnership webinar brought Black business leaders, City representatives and coalition members together to discuss how walking and bicycling relate to community health and local business.

This is a historical campaign summary. The earlier rack application is no longer presented as an open offer. To discuss a new neighborhood or business partnership, [get involved with the coalition](/get-involved/).
