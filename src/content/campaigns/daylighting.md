## What is daylighting?
Daylighting keeps the space near a crossing clear so people approaching an intersection have a better view of one another. Depending on the location, a design might use paint, curb extensions, posts or carefully placed planting.

## A neighborhood demonstration
The coalition is working with neighbors in the New Marigny / South Seventh Ward area on a demonstration at **St. Anthony Street and Henriette Delille Street**. Community input helped identify visibility and crossing needs.

The image above shows a **design concept**, not a completed installation. As of November 18, 2026, the team was coordinating permits, materials and a community design event. A build date will be shared when confirmed.

## How you can help
Help with neighborhood outreach, creative design, materials or a future volunteer day. Share what you notice when you walk, roll, bike or drive through the intersection.
