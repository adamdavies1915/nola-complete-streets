## Implementation needs an owner
The coalition advocates for clear responsibilities, coordination across agencies and a public record of progress. These are practical needs when a street project touches transit, drainage, trees, sidewalks and road design.

## Our current priorities
We are following the City's complete streets implementation process, supporting public access to project information and working on an update to our 2025 report card.

Bring a project question to a meeting, help interpret a public dataset or share an experience that a citywide measure might miss.
