The coalition's 2020–2021 activation mini-grants helped neighborhood groups connect complete streets with local priorities, from tree cover to family rides and access to fresh food.

## What communities made possible
The original program page documented projects including:

- **SOUL:** planting 105 trees along Newton Street in Old Algiers, with signs explaining their benefits.
- **Positive Impact and Uplift NOLA:** an Algiers Peace Ride introducing neighbors to bike infrastructure.
- **Sprout NOLA:** market discounts for visitors arriving on foot, by bicycle or by transit.
- **Heart N Hands:** health education and opportunities to walk, run and ride.
- **BAWSE Bike Collective:** bicycle lights and bells for community members.
- **Old Algiers Main Street:** an open house about Newton Street changes and neighborhood access.

## Bring that approach to your event
These are past projects, and the original grant round is closed. The [Complete Streets Event Partnership](/get-involved/certified-events/) carries forward the idea of planning for how people reach and experience an event.
