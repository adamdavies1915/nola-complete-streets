## A street that connects neighborhoods
St. Claude connects homes, businesses, schools, transit and nightlife. People need to reach those places safely, whether walking, riding a bike, taking the bus or driving.

The coalition supports the Safer St. Claude effort alongside neighborhood advocates, Bike Easy and the Bywater Marigny business community. The work connects immediate concerns with a longer-term vision for the corridor.

## What we are asking for
- Crossings people can see and comfortably use.
- Better visibility, lighting and access at intersections.
- A connected bikeway with appropriate protection.
- Transit access and sidewalks that work together.
- A clear public process, with updates residents can follow.

## Where the work stands
An October 20, 2026 road safety audit brought agency and community perspectives to the corridor. The coalition is following the next steps for short-term improvements and longer-term planning.
