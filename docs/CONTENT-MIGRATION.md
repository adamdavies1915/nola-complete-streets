## Previously retained

Coalition identity and mission/vision; source report card, policy memo and strategic plan PDFs; selected original photographs; summaries of Connect the Crescent, Moving New Orleans Bikes, daylighting and Safer St. Claude. Most page copy is rewritten, not copied verbatim.

## Remaining scope and editorial choices

- The entire paginated news archive has not been inventoried or migrated. This pass reviewed the visible news index and selected substantive articles.
- Old event reminders, volunteer dates, election deadlines and closed applications are omitted from current actions.
- Original neighborhood maps are not used for navigation. Historical guides link to Bike Streets and agency project sources.
- The historical member/supporter directory does not establish current membership. Draft roster entries remain unpublished pending confirmation.
- Additional original photographs, testimonials and detailed construction worksheets remain available for a later archival pass; this pass adds text, not new imagery.
- Unknown campaign dates are labeled “Campaign archive” rather than inferred.
